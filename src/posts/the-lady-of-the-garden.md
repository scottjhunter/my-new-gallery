  <h1>The Lady of the Garden</h1>
  <figure class="blog-page-image">
    <img src="/images/snail.jpg" alt="Illustration of an armored snail beneath the moon beside a castle, accompanying a poem about patience and quiet strength.">
  </figure>
  <p>
    Beneath the moon's soft silver veil,<br>
    Glides the armored queen, the noble snail.<br>
    Her shield, a spiral etched with care,<br>
    More radiant than Arthur's wear.
  </p>
  <p>
    No clang of steel, no battle cry,<br>
    Yet courage gleams beneath the sky.<br>
    A fortress borne upon her back,<br>
    A steadfast guard against attack.
  </p>
  <p>
    Through storms and trials, she holds her ground,<br>
    A quiet strength, profound, unbound.<br>
    With patience forged in nature's art,<br>
    She charts her course, a work of heart.
  </p>
  <p>
    Oh, snail, your grace, your calm, your might,<br>
    A humble queen in soft moonlight.<br>
    Teach us all to wear our shell,<br>
    To carry home and guard it well.
  </p>
  <p><em>Poem by Scott J. Hunter.</em></p>
